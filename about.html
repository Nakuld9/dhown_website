About DHOWN Hosiery Mills

A dedicated knitting manufacturer from the heart of India's textile capital — Ludhiana, Punjab.

Who We Are

Manufacturing Excellence from Ludhiana

DHOWN Hosiery Mills is a professional circular knitting manufacturer operating out of Ludhiana, Punjab — a city that has defined India's textile industry for generations. We produce high-quality knitted fabrics for garment manufacturers, brands, and exporters across India and beyond.

Our facility runs on modern circular knitting machines across two production shifts every day — ensuring continuous output, consistent quality, and reliable delivery for our partners.

We are not traders. We are manufacturers. Every roll of fabric that leaves DHOWN is produced in-house on our own machines, inspected by our team, and packed to your specification. This gives us complete control over quality, lead times, and pricing — benefits that pass directly to you.

What Sets Us Apart

Own Factory, Own Machines
Direct mill pricing with zero middleman markup. You get the best rate because you're dealing with the producer.
Digital Production Tracking
Our facility uses a live MES system to monitor every machine in real time — RPM, production, quality, and downtime — ensuring nothing slips through.
Specification Flexibility
Custom GSM, gauge, yarn blend, and width — we produce to your exact requirements rather than pushing standard stock.
Long-Term Partnerships
We build lasting relationships with our buyers — understanding your seasonal needs, scheduling ahead, and growing together.

Our Values

What We Stand For

The principles that guide every decision we make — from machine settings to business relationships.

Precision

We believe consistency is quality. Every machine is calibrated, every roll is measured, and every delivery is checked before it leaves our gates.

Integrity

Honest pricing, honest timelines, and honest communication. We would rather lose an order than promise something we cannot deliver.

Reliability

Our partners count on us for regular supply. We invest in scheduling, machine maintenance, and staffing to ensure we never let an order slip.

Growth Mindset

We continuously upgrade our machines, adopt new yarn compositions, and improve our processes to stay ahead of the market and serve you better.

Location

Based in Ludhiana — India's Textile Capital

Ludhiana is the manufacturing powerhouse of North India's textile industry. Being based here gives us access to premium yarn suppliers, skilled labour, and a deep network of processing and finishing partners — advantages we pass on to our customers.
